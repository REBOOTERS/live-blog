Gif Factory Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026  |  Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Gif Factory ("this app") is an image / GIF processing tool that runs entirely on your device. It provides features such as GIF reverse, photo area blur, video delogo, photo filters, GIF to WebP conversion, and sketch / pencil filters.

We take your personal information seriously. This policy explains what information this app processes, which permissions it requests, and your rights. Please read it carefully before use; by using this app you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this policy.

Our core promise: this app does not connect to the internet, does not upload, and does not collect. The photos / videos you pick are processed only in your device's memory, and results stay on your device. We have no servers and no analytics, ads, or push SDKs.

2. Information We Collect and Process

This app does not collect or upload any personal information and has no network capability (no internet permission is requested). The following information is generated and used only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Media files you pick: photos / videos you choose through the system album picker are used solely for local processing (delogo, filters, reverse, etc.). Once processing finishes, the app does not keep additional copies (except the cache and history described below).
  • Processing results: generated images / videos are first stored in the app's private cache directory for previewing, sharing, or saving.
  • Local history: for your convenience, the app keeps a local database record of processed files (path, size, time). This record stays on your device and can be deleted at any time (see Section 5, "Your Rights").
  • Preferences: theme and feature settings, stored locally on the device.

3. Permissions

This app requests the following permissions only when a feature requires them. All permissions can be denied or revoked at any time in system settings; denying one makes the corresponding feature unavailable without affecting the rest:

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Local storage: processing results (private cache directory), local history (private database), and preferences are all stored in the app's private space on the device.
  • Saving to the album: results are written to the system album only when you tap "save"; after that, the system album manages them.
  • Cache clearing: result caches can be cleared via system "Settings → Apps → Gif Factory → Clear cache", or are removed automatically on uninstall.
  • History deletion: you can delete any history entry — singly or in batches — inside "My Files" in the app; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

5. Your Rights

Because this app does not upload information to any server, controlling your data means controlling your device:

  1. Know and refuse: every permission request has a clear purpose. You may deny any of them and revoke granted ones at any time in system settings.
  2. Delete: delete any history entry inside the app, or remove all local data at once by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  3. Export: everything you process belongs to you — export it to the system album or other apps at any time via the in-app share / save functions.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

This app does not share, transfer, or disclose your information to any third party, except when initiated by you:

  • You share a processing result to another app (e.g. WeChat, QQ) through the system share sheet; the chosen app then receives the content, and this app takes no part in what happens next.
  • You save a processing result to the system album.
  • Mandatory disclosure required by applicable laws or competent authorities.

7. Third-Party SDKs

This app integrates no analytics, advertising, push, or tracking SDKs of any kind. No third party collects information about you through this app.

8. Protection of Minors

This app does not collect any information from minors. Minors should use it under a guardian's supervision, with the guardian making authorization decisions on their behalf.

9. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features evolve. Updated versions will be published on the app's listing page or in the app, with the effective date noted. When a change materially affects permission usage, we will prominently re-notify you — for example in the permission dialog — before the permission is requested.
